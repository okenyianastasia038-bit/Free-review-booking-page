# Brand Concept: Anastasia A.O

This document describes the brand behind the Free Website Review booking page: what it stands for, how it looks and how it should feel. It guides every page, post and message that carries the Anastasia A.O name.

---

## 1. Brand summary

| | |
|---|---|
| **Brand name** | Anastasia A.O |
| **What it is** | A one-person UI/UX design studio for online stores |
| **What it offers** | A free 30-minute website and conversion review, followed by a paid full-site redesign |
| **Who it serves** | Owners of small and mid-sized online stores selling fashion, food, beauty and other products, mostly outside Nigeria |
| **Core promise** | Your store gets visitors. Let’s turn more of them into buyers. |
| **Brand line** | No pressure and no jargon. Just honest feedback from a UI/UX designer who cares about your sales. |

## 2. The brand idea: “The case of the missing sales”

Every store owner who comes to the page has the same mystery: people visit, add items, browse, and then disappear. The brand treats lost sales like a case to solve.

- **The clues** are the everyday problems shoppers run into: a cart left behind, a checkout that confuses, a page that will not load, a phone screen covered in pop-ups.
- **The detective** is Anastasia, who looks at the store through a shopper’s eyes and connects the clues.
- **The solution** is clear, specific fixes, and if the owner wants them done for them, a redesign.

This idea shows up most clearly in the pinboard section of the page, where red threads connect five frustrations to one answer. It also shapes the language: we *spot*, *find*, *uncover* and *fix*. We never blame the owner.

## 3. Mission, vision and values

**Mission:** Help online store owners understand why shoppers leave, and fix it with design that is clear, fast and kind to the people using it.

**Vision:** A world where small online stores compete with the big ones on experience, because good design is no longer something only large brands can afford.

**Values**

1. **Honesty first.** Say what is wrong, even when it is not what the owner hoped to hear, and say what is working too.
2. **Plain language.** If a store owner needs a dictionary to understand the advice, the advice has failed.
3. **Shoppers at the centre.** Every recommendation starts with what a real shopper sees, thinks and feels.
4. **Proof over promises.** Show the problem, show the fix, then measure the change.
5. **Generosity.** The free review is genuinely useful on its own, whether or not the owner hires us.

## 4. Positioning

**For** online store owners who get traffic but not enough sales,
**Anastasia A.O is** a UI/UX designer and conversion partner
**who** finds exactly where shoppers get stuck and redesigns the store around them.
**Unlike** large agencies with long sales calls and vague reports, or cheap template sellers who change colours and call it a redesign,
**Anastasia A.O** starts with a free, specific review and offers a fixed-price redesign built on what the review found.

## 5. Brand personality

If the brand were a person, she would be the friend who happens to be a designer: warm, sharp, a little playful, and always honest.

## 6. Visual identity

### Colour

The palette balances warmth and trust with a playful edge.

| Colour | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Cream | `#FEF7E5` | The page. Warm, calm and paper-like |
| Burgundy | `#7A1F3D` | The main brand colour. Buttons, accents, the signature wordmark |
| Black | `#141414` | Headlines, strong sections and the footer |
| Butter yellow | `#F6D77B` | Playful supporting colour for cards and doodles |
| Blush pink | `#F4BFD0` | Playful supporting colour for cards and shapes |
| Thread red | `#D7263D` | Only for the pinboard pins and threads |

Burgundy carries the brand. Butter and blush appear in small, joyful doses and never compete with the call to action. Full details are in [DESIGN_SYSTEM.md](DESIGN_SYSTEM.md#colour).

### Typography

| Typeface | Personality | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Young Serif | Warm, editorial, credible | Headlines, card titles, the brand line |
| Figtree | Friendly, clear, modern | Body text, labels, buttons |
| Caveat | Handwritten, human | Pinboard captions and margin notes only |
| Anton | Bold, poster-like | The oversized name in the footer only |

### Brand marks

- **Wordmark:** “Anastasia A.O” set in Young Serif, followed by a small burgundy four-point sparkle that slowly rotates on the web.
- **Signature wordmark:** “ANASTASIA A.O” set in Anton across the full width of the footer, with the first A tilted, like a stamp at the end of a letter.
- **Sparkle:** A four-point star used as a bullet, a decoration and a favicon. It stands for the moment a problem becomes clear.
- **Favicon:** A cream sparkle on a burgundy rounded square.

### Graphic language

- **Hand-drawn line illustrations** in black with 2 to 2.5px strokes and round ends, filled with flat brand colours. Subjects are everyday store moments: carts, checkouts, phones, product pages, doors.
- **The pinboard:** polaroid-style panels, red pins, red threads, washi tape and margin notes. Used for storytelling about problems and solutions.
- **Doodles:** squiggles, hearts, sparkles and scribbled circles, used sparingly to add a human touch around key content.
- **The asterisk:** a small asterisk after card titles hints that there is more detail behind the headline.

### Photography

- Use one real, recent portrait of Anastasia with natural light and a friendly, direct expression.
- Crop it to a rounded square or circle.
- Do not use stock photos of people pretending to be clients or team members.
- Use screenshots of real stores only with the owner’s permission, and blur customer data.

### Motion

Motion should feel like a friendly nudge. It is springy and quick, never slow or dramatic.

- Elements rise into place with a soft overshoot.
- Cards lift and tilt slightly when touched or hovered.
- Threads draw themselves to show how problems connect.
- All motion stops when a visitor’s device asks for reduced motion.

## 7. Voice in one line

Talk like a trusted designer friend explaining your store over coffee: clear, kind, specific and honest. Full rules are in [CONTENT_GUIDELINES.md](CONTENT_GUIDELINES.md).

## 8. Brand dos and don’ts

| Do | Don’t |
|---|---|
| Lead with the store owner’s problem in their own words | Lead with design terms like “information architecture” |
| Keep burgundy for the most important action on the screen | Use thread red for buttons or text |
| Use one doodle or sparkle to add warmth near key content | Scatter decoration everywhere |
| Show a real face and a real name | Hide behind a faceless agency voice |
| Promise specific, honest outcomes | Promise guaranteed sales or “10x growth” |
| Keep the cream background as the base | Place burgundy text on butter or blush where contrast drops |
